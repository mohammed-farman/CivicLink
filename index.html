About CivicLink

A simple idea: make it easier for people to understand civic problems and easier for communities to report them.

CivicLink is a community-focused civic platform designed to connect people with information about the issues around them.

The idea begins in Andhra Pradesh and can eventually expand to communities across India. People should be able to report problems such as damaged roads, broken streetlights, waste-management problems, water issues and other civic concerns from one simple platform.

CivicLink is not only about reporting problems. It is also about awareness. By showing civic updates, community issues and improvements, the platform can help people learn more about how their communities are developing.

The long-term vision is to create a system where a reported issue can be recorded, reviewed, assigned to the appropriate team and eventually marked as resolved.

1. Report People submit a civic issue.
2. Review The report is checked.
3. Action The responsible team can respond.
4. Resolve The issue can be updated as resolved.
